📖 Guide

How It Works

Three simple steps to translate anything.

01

Type Your Text

Enter any text in the source panel. Use auto-detect or manually select your source language.

02

Choose Target

Pick your target language from 50+ options. Use swap to quickly reverse the translation direction.

03

Translate!

Click Translate or press Ctrl+Enter. Listen to the result, copy, download, or save to history.
